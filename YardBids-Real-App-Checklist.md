# YardBids real app checklist

## Already designed and prototyped

- Social, swipeable auction discovery
- Live bids, countdowns, chat, sharing, and watchlists
- Seller listings with photos or short videos
- Local pickup, Safe Meetup locations, scheduling, and reporting
- Shipping checkout and delivery tracking
- Ratings, reviews, notifications, following sellers, and seller payouts

## Behind-the-scenes foundation completed

- User and seller profiles
- Listings, auctions, bids, chats, and orders
- Atomic bid handling with final-minute extensions
- Payments, payouts, ratings, reports, watchlists, follows, and notifications
- Protected payment, pickup, delivery, and dispute state changes

## What connects it to the real world

1. Add user sign-in and phone verification.
2. Connect the database schema to a hosted PostgreSQL database.
3. Connect a payment provider for buyer charges and seller payouts.
4. Connect a shipping-label provider for prepaid labels and tracking.
5. Add a map provider for real Safe Meetup suggestions.
6. Connect real-time updates so all bidders see bids and chat immediately.
7. Add moderation tools for reports and prohibited-item review.

## Safest launch order

Start with one local area and public pickup only. Once auctions, ratings, and support are running smoothly, add shipping. Nationwide shipping and seller livestreaming can come after that.

## Core launch promise

**Find it. Bid it. Meet safely.**

YardBids helps people turn everyday household items into quick, social, trusted sales—nearby or delivered.
