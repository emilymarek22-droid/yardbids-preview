Selling guidelines

Keep YardBids useful and safe

List everyday household items honestly, with clear photos and accurate condition details.

Great things to list

Home goods, furniture, tools, toys, and electronics
Make sure items are legal to sell, accurately described, and ready for safe pickup or shipping.

Not allowed

Unsafe or restricted items
Do not list weapons, illegal goods, recalled or hazardous products, counterfeit items, stolen property, or personal services.
Respect privacy
Never post home addresses, phone numbers, or sensitive personal information in a listing or public comment.
See something concerning?Use Report a problem from Safety & support. YardBids can review the listing and keep payment protected when an order is involved.

Help center

Common questions

Quick answers for bidding, payments, pickups, and shipping.

When is my bid charged?
Only if you win. Your payment is completed after the auction closes and you choose pickup or shipping.
How do safe meetups work?
Choose a well-lit public place, coordinate in YardBids chat, inspect the item, and confirm only after you receive it.
What if something goes wrong?
Use Report a problem before confirming pickup or delivery. Your payment stays protected while YardBids reviews the order.
How does shipping work?
The seller receives a prepaid label after payment. Track the package in YardBids and confirm receipt when it arrives.
